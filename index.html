Report Flow

Nine steps. One complete report every day.

Weekly duty teachers fill in a report for each day — Monday to Friday. Every step is short and clear.

  1. 01

    Teacher Attendance

    Absent teachers and the reason — sick leave, courses, official duty.

  2. 02

    Student Attendance

    Attendance for each class; percentages calculated automatically.

  3. 03

    Sick & Clinic

    Unwell students, sick bay treatment or clinic visits.

  4. 04

    Grounds & Facilities

    Cleanliness, toilets, cafeteria, fences & security.

  5. 05

    Visitors

    Name, organisation, purpose and time in & out.

  6. 06

    Discipline

    Lateness, truancy, uniform and action taken.

  7. 07

    Daily Activities

    Assemblies, programmes, CCAs and meetings.

  8. 08

    Management Approval

    Administrators review, comment, and approve or return.

  9. 09

    Done

    A complete summary — ready to print & file.
